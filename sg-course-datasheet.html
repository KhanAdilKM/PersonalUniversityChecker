How to read this table — sources, assumptions and caveats
  • GPA p10 / p90 — the polytechnic GPA of the 10th and 90th percentile applicant who was offered a place in AY2025, out of 4.00. Figures in amber with an EST tag are estimates, not published data (see below).
  • Estimated GPAs. NUS, NTU and SMU suppress a GPA where the polytechnic sample is small (marked "#"). Those were estimated from the programme's A-Level profile and from sibling programmes in the same school. SUTD publishes no IGP at all, so all five SUTD programmes are estimated on a 3.40–3.70 scale, ranked by competitiveness and graduate salary. SIT publishes offer rates by GPA band rather than percentiles, so every SIT figure is derived from those bands — the raw percentages are printed under each SIT course so you can check the reasoning.
  • GES — Graduate Employment Survey 2025, conducted November 2025, roughly six months after final examinations. "Employed" is the proportion of the labour force currently employed; "Full-time perm." is the narrower full-time permanent share; "Median gross" is the median gross monthly salary of full-time permanently employed graduates. Where MOE published no figure the cell reads NULL and the reason is given under the course name. Nothing was invented.
  • Total fees — tuition only, for a non-ASEAN international student who takes up the MOE Tuition Grant, inclusive of 9% GST, at the AY2026 published rate multiplied by the normal course duration. NUS, NTU, SMU and SUTD charge per academic year; SIT charges per credit, so SIT totals are the per-credit rate times the programme's total credits. Excludes miscellaneous fees, housing and living costs. The grant carries a three-year obligation to work for a Singapore-registered employer.
  • Distance — straight-line kilometres from Jurong East MRT to the campus the programme is taught at. The minutes column is an approximate door-to-door public-transport time and is indicative only. Campuses used: NUS Kent Ridge (Law at Bukit Timah), NTU Jurong West (Medicine at Novena, NIE programmes at the NIE campus), SMU City Campus, SUTD Somapah Road, SIT Punggol.
  • Fees ÷ salary — total tuition divided by the median gross monthly starting salary: roughly how many months of first-job gross pay the degree costs. Lower is better value on this one narrow measure. Blank where there is no salary figure.
  • Both IGP and GES are backward-looking. Meeting a past percentile guarantees nothing, and salary reflects a cohort that graduated years before anyone reading this would.
